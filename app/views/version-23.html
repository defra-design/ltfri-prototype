Flood depth Version 23.0

Accessibility fix for depth gauge contrast ratios
Accessibility

Version 23.0 introduces a critical accessibility fix for low-vision users on the rivers and sea depth page. The contrast ratios for the depth gauge meter graphics (for 20cm, 30cm, and 60cm depths) have been updated to meet the WCAG 3:1 contrast ratio threshold against both the white page background and the blue/grey elements.

V23 changes
  • Updated grey against white and grey against blue contrast ratios to satisfy the WCAG 3:1 threshold on the depth page.
