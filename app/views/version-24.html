Taking action (Phase 1) Version 24.0

Showing different presentation styles for the different flood types.
Ready for dev

Version 24 introduces a clear single risk indicator alongside the address summary, It also adds a prominent "Get free flood warnings" action card or "Prepare for flooding".

V24.0 changes

Version A (Rivers and sea – Get flood warnings)

  • Show the 'Get flood warnings' action card when rivers and the sea is the highest risk, and the address sits within a Flood Warning (FW) or Severe Flood Warning (SFW) area.

Version B (Surface water – Get flood warnings)

  • Show the 'Get flood warnings' action card when surface water is the highest risk, and the address sits within a Flood Warning (FW) or Severe Flood Warning (SFW) area.

Version C (Surface water – Prepare for flooding)

  • Show the 'Prepare for flooding' action component when surface water is the highest risk, but the address sits outside a Flood Warning (FW) or Severe Flood Warning (SFW) coverage area.

Version D (Very low risk – No action)

  • No action component displayed when both rivers and sea and surface water risks are rated as 'Very low'.
